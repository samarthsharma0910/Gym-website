Our Story

We built what we
couldn't find.

IronForge was founded in 2009 by competitive powerlifters who were frustrated by gyms that couldn't keep up. Fifteen years later, we're still the benchmark.

Founded 2009

From a warehouse to
the city's best gym.

IronForge started as a 4,000 sq ft bare-concrete warehouse in the warehouse district with two power racks, a barbell set, and the ambition to build something that took training seriously.

Our founders — Marcus Reid and co-owner Alicia Vance — had competed at national level in powerlifting and were convinced that most commercial gyms were designed around aesthetics, not performance. They built IronForge to change that.

Today, we operate across 38,000 sq ft of purpose-built training space with over 12,000 members, a team of 48 specialist coaches, and programs that have helped members achieve everything from their first pull-up to national competition titles.

Mission

Elevate every member. Every session.

Our mission is simple: create an environment where people can genuinely improve their physical capabilities, regardless of their starting point. We believe fitness is a practice, not a destination — and we exist to support that practice at the highest possible level.

We reject the "average gym" model. There's no loud music to mask inadequate training. No rows of duplicate machines bought by committee. No personal trainers who hand out cookie-cutter plans. At IronForge, everything — from equipment procurement to coach hiring to program design — is held to a single standard: does it produce results?

Vision

The standard for serious training.

We want IronForge to be the name people think of when they decide they're ready to stop dabbling and start training seriously. We're not for everyone — and we're proud of that.

Our vision is to expand our model thoughtfully — keeping the culture, the standards, and the community intact as we grow. Bigger doesn't mean better unless the quality stays uncompromised.
